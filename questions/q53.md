**English transcription of the question:** Let the buffers of main memory maintained by the DBMS be called cache. Some of these rules are: steal, no-steal, force, and no-force. Regarding this topic, analyze the assertions below and select the correct alternative.

I. The force rule determines that all pages updated by a transaction are immediately written to secondary memory before the transaction commits.  
II. Under the no-force rule, a page updated by a committed transaction may still be in cache when another transaction needs to update it.  
III. Under the no-steal rule, UNDO-type operations will never be necessary during the recovery process.

A) All assertions are correct.  
B) All assertions are incorrect.  
C) Only assertions I and II are correct.  
D) Only assertions I and III are correct.  
E) Only assertions II and III are correct.
