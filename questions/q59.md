**English transcription of the question:** A large logistics company plans to replace its fleet management system. Due to the high complexity and risk of a complete and simultaneous replacement, management chose a strategy based on delivering operational versions of the software in parts. The plan consists of developing and delivering the system as a series of predefined functionalities: the first delivery refers to a product with the essential tracking functionalities; the second will add the maintenance management module; and the third will implement route optimization. Each delivery refers to complete and usable software that integrates with the previous ones. Which software development life-cycle model does this approach describe?

A) Spiral Model.  
B) Incremental Model.  
C) Waterfall Model.  
D) Prototyping.  
E) V-Model.
