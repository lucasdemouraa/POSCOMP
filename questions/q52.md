**English transcription of the question:** Regarding database transaction schedules, consider that the Initial State is the state of the database before the execution of the schedule, and the Final State is the state of the database after the execution of the schedule. A nonserial schedule S of n transactions is said to be serializable if:

I. It is conflict-equivalent to some serial schedule of the same n transactions.  
II. The final state of S is the same as the final state of some serial schedule of the same n transactions, with respect to at least one initial state.  
III. The final state of S is the same as the final state of some serial schedule of the same n transactions, with respect to all initial states.

Which are correct?

A) I only.  
B) II only.  
C) III only.  
D) I and II only.  
E) I and III only.
